Quick answers

  • Can diabetic patients get dental implants?

    Usually yes, if blood sugar is under control. Diabetes slows healing, so an HbA1c test is checked before implant surgery and extra precautions are taken. Uncontrolled diabetes is brought under control first. Watch the video.

  • Can I get dental implants if I take blood thinners?

    Being on blood thinners does not by itself rule out implants. We plan the surgery with your cardiologist or physician and get written medical clearance before treatment. Never stop a blood thinner on your own. Watch the video.

  • Is there an age limit for dental implants?

    There is no fixed upper age. Overall health, the amount of bone, healing ability and medical history matter more than age. Implants in growing teenagers are usually delayed until the jaw stops growing. Watch the video.

  • Why is a CBCT scan needed before an implant?

    A normal X-ray is flat. A CBCT scan shows the jawbone, nerve and sinus in 3D, so the implant size, position and angle can be planned safely before surgery. At our clinic the CBCT scan is included in the implant price. Watch the video.

  • What is guided implant surgery?

    The implant is planned on the CBCT and intraoral scans first, and a 3D-printed surgical guide then carries that plan into the mouth. It helps place the implant at the planned position, depth and angle. Watch the video.

  • Do I need a bone graft before a dental implant?

    Only if the CBCT scan shows there is not enough bone where the implant must go. If you need one, it is written into your estimate before treatment starts. In full-arch cases, tilted implants can often avoid a graft. Watch the short or the full answer on bone grafting for dental implants.

  • Can an implant be placed on the same day as the extraction?

    Sometimes. An immediate implant is possible when the bone is healthy and there is no active infection. Otherwise it is safer to let the site heal first. The CBCT scan helps decide. Watch the video or read more on immediate dental implants.

  • Is an implant better than a bridge for one missing tooth?

    A bridge needs the neighbouring teeth to be cut down to hold it, while an implant replaces the missing tooth on its own. An implant is not automatically better for everyone: bone, the neighbouring teeth and your bite decide. Watch the video.

  • Why are some dental implants much cheaper than others?

    A low price often covers only the implant screw. The result depends on the whole system: implant brand, planning, guided surgery, a custom abutment, the crown and follow-up care. Compare full prices, not the screw alone. Watch the video.

  • What is an implant abutment?

    The abutment is the connector between the implant in the bone and the crown you see. We use custom abutments milled from titanium grade 5 for a precise fit. Watch the video.

  • Can a dental implant fail years later?

    It can, usually because of gum infection around the implant (peri-implantitis), heavy biting forces or poor cleaning. Bleeding or swollen gums around the implant, pain, a loose crown or a bad taste are reasons to see your dentist early. Watch the video.
