Basal implants: cost, and who they suit

Dr Pushkar Sobti
All-on-X implantologist, Delhi

A lot of the patients who ask me about basal implants have already heard "no" somewhere else. Usually it went something like this: there isn't enough bone, you'll need a graft first, come back in six months. For someone who has worn a loose denture for years, that's hard news.

Basal implants are one way around it. They aren't the right answer for everyone, and they cost more per jaw than people expect when they first hear the word "implant", so this post covers both.

What makes them different

When a tooth is lost, the bone that held it slowly shrinks. After years of missing teeth or a denture pressing on the gum, the upper layer of the jaw can get too thin or too low for a regular implant.

Deeper down there is a layer called basal bone. It is dense, and it doesn't shrink the way the upper bone does. Basal implants are designed to anchor in that deeper layer. Because they hold firmly from the start, a fixed set of teeth can go on within 72 hours, and there's no graft to wait for.

Who they suit

I suggest basal implants only when regular implants are not possible, or would need grafting the patient doesn't want. In practice that is mostly:

  • people who have worn a full denture for many years and lost a lot of bone
  • people who have been told they need a sinus lift or a large graft, and want to avoid it
  • people who need the whole jaw done and can't spend months without fixed teeth

If your scan shows that ordinary implants or All-on-4 can work, I'll tell you, because for most people those are the better long-term choice. Basal implants are a solution for a specific problem, not an upgrade.

Basal implants cost: what you are paying for

At our Rajouri Garden clinic, basal implants start from ₹2,00,000 per jaw. That figure is for a full jaw, not one tooth, and it includes the fixed teeth fitted within 72 hours.

What moves the final price up or down:

  • how many implants your jaw needs
  • the material of the fixed teeth
  • whether one jaw or both are being done

You get the full cost in writing after the CBCT scan, before anything starts. The consultation is ₹700, and the scan is included in the treatment price.

If you're comparing this with a single-tooth figure from somewhere, it can look like a big number. It's a different treatment. For a clear side-by-side of single implants, full mouth and basal options, see our dental implant cost in Delhi page.

Finding out if they're right for you

The only real way to know is a CBCT scan. It shows exactly how much bone is left and where. If you already have a scan or an OPG X-ray from another clinic, send it on WhatsApp. I can usually tell you from that whether regular implants are still possible, or whether basal implants are worth discussing.

Questions

  • How much do basal implants cost in Delhi?

    At Dr Sobti's Dental Care in Rajouri Garden, basal implants start from ₹2,00,000 per jaw, including fixed teeth within 72 hours. The final price depends on the number of implants and the material of the teeth, and is given in writing after a CBCT scan.

  • Do basal implants need a bone graft?

    No. They anchor in the deeper basal bone, which is why they are used where the upper bone has shrunk too much for regular implants.

  • Are basal implants better than regular implants?

    Not in general. They solve a specific problem: too little bone. If regular implants or All-on-4 can work for you, they are usually the better long-term choice.
