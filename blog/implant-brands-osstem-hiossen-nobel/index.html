Osstem, Hiossen or Nobel: does the implant brand matter?

Dr Pushkar Sobti
All-on-X implantologist, Delhi

Patients often ask me which implant is "the best one". It's a fair question, and the honest answer is that a well-known brand placed badly will do worse than a good brand placed well. The planning and the surgery matter more than the name on the box.

That said, the brand isn't nothing. It is the part of your tooth that stays in your jaw for the rest of your life, so it's worth knowing what you're choosing.

The three I use

I have placed more than 5,000 implants since 2009, and at our Rajouri Garden clinic I work mainly with three brands. Each has many years of clinical use behind it, which is the first thing I look for.

Osstem is a Korean company and one of the most widely used implant systems in the world. Its CA implant is what I use for most single teeth and many full-mouth cases.

Hiossen is Osstem's sister brand, set up for the US market. It's a good middle option for patients who want that step up.

Nobel Biocare is Swiss, with one of the longest research histories in implant dentistry. Nobel Active with its TiUltra surface is the premium choice.

Your written plan names which one you're getting.

What the brand changes

The brand decides the implant's design and surface: the thread shape, how it grips soft or hard bone, and how the surface encourages bone to grow onto it. In difficult bone, some designs give a little more confidence at the moment of placement.

It also decides parts and support. An implant from a large, established company can be restored and repaired for decades, because the screws and components will still be made. An unknown brand from a supplier that disappears in five years can leave you with a tooth no one can service.

What the brand doesn't change

It doesn't change the planning, the guided surgery, the custom abutment or the crown. At our clinic those are the same whichever implant you choose, and they are all included in the price.

How the price differs

A single implant with everything included starts from ₹35,000 with Osstem, ₹45,000 with Hiossen and ₹60,000 with Nobel. The difference is the cost of the implant itself and its parts. For most people, Osstem does the job very well, and I'll tell you if your case is one where I'd suggest something else.

Questions

  • Which implant brand is best?

    There isn't one best brand for everyone. Osstem, Hiossen and Nobel all have long clinical records. The planning and surgery matter more than the brand, and your bone and budget decide which one suits you.

  • Why does a Nobel implant cost more?

    The implant and its parts cost more to buy. At Dr Sobti's Dental Care in Rajouri Garden, a single implant starts from ₹35,000 with Osstem, ₹45,000 with Hiossen and ₹60,000 with Nobel, with the abutment, crown and guided surgery included in each.

    Yes. Your written plan names the brand before treatment starts.
