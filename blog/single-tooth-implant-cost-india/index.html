Single tooth implant cost in India: what the price should include

Dr Pushkar Sobti
All-on-X implantologist, Delhi

Almost every week someone shows me a quote from another clinic and asks why ours is higher. Sometimes the other quote is ₹15,000, sometimes ₹20,000. My answer is always the same: before comparing two numbers, check that they are paying for the same things.

The single tooth implant cost in India varies a lot, and dental implants in Delhi are no different. Most of that gap comes from what is left out of the cheaper quote, not from one clinic being greedy.

What one implant tooth is made of

A missing tooth replaced with an implant has three parts. The implant itself is a small titanium screw that sits in the bone. On top of it goes the abutment, which comes up through the gum. On the abutment sits the crown, the part you see and chew with.

A quote that names only "implant" may cover just the screw. The abutment and crown then come later, as separate bills.

What our price includes

At our Rajouri Garden clinic a single implant starts at ₹35,000, and that is the full tooth. It covers:

  • a CBCT scan, so I can see the bone in 3D before deciding anything
  • a digital plan and guided surgery, using a 3D-printed guide
  • an Osstem implant (Hiossen and Nobel are also available, from ₹45,000 and ₹60,000)
  • a custom abutment, made for your gum shape
  • a zirconia crown from our own lab

There is nothing extra to pay for the parts on that list. If your scan shows you need a bone graft, that is written into your estimate before we start, so you know the full figure on day one.

Where cheap quotes usually save money

When I look at a low quote, these are the things I check for:

  • No CBCT scan, or a scan charged separately. Without one, the implant is placed by feel.
  • A stock abutment instead of a custom one. It works, but the crown can sit less naturally at the gum.
  • A metal-ceramic crown instead of zirconia, or the crown not included at all.
  • An implant brand with little long-term research behind it.
  • A free first visit, then charges for each step after.

None of this means a cheaper clinic is doing bad work. It means the number on the first quote is not the number you will pay.

Questions worth asking any clinic

  1. Is the crown included? What material?
  2. Is the abutment custom or stock?
  3. Which implant brand, and can I see the card with its batch number?
  4. Is the CBCT scan included?
  5. If I need a bone graft, how much is it?

If you have a quote from somewhere else, send it to us on WhatsApp and I'll tell you honestly what it covers and what it doesn't. You can see the full price list on our dental implant cost in Delhi page.

Questions

  • How much does a single tooth implant cost in India?

    Quotes range widely, from about ₹15,000 to over ₹60,000. The difference is mostly what's included. At Dr Sobti's Dental Care in Rajouri Garden, a single implant with CBCT, guided surgery, custom abutment and zirconia crown starts at ₹35,000.

  • Is the crown included in the implant price?

    In our clinic, yes. At many clinics it isn't, so ask before you compare quotes.

  • Why is a bone graft charged separately?

    Not everyone needs one. If your CBCT shows you do, it's added to your written estimate before treatment starts.
