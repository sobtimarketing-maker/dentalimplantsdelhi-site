Three parts: implant, abutment, teeth

  • The implant

    A titanium screw that sits in the jawbone like a root. We use Osstem, Hiossen and Nobel, and place titanium implants only.

  • The abutment

    The connector between the implant and the tooth. A custom abutment is shaped for your gum and milled in titanium in our lab, so the crown sits at the right height and angle.

  • The prosthesis

    The crown, bridge or full arch of teeth. Its fit on the implants matters as much as the implant itself, which is why we make it here.

Types of teeth on implants

  • Single implant crown

    One tooth on one implant. Zirconia on a custom titanium abutment. From ₹35,000 including the implant. See implant prices.

  • Implant bridge

    Three or more missing teeth carried on two or more implants, without cutting the neighbouring teeth.

  • Bridge with pink gum

    When bone and gum have shrunk, pink ceramic or composite replaces the lost gum so the teeth do not look long.

  • All-on-X full arch

    Every tooth in one jaw on four to six implants, screwed in and never taken out at home. From ₹2,00,000 per jaw. See full mouth implants.

  • Implant-supported denture

    A denture that clips onto implants or a bar. It stays put while you eat but can be taken out to clean.

  • Same-day temporary teeth

    A PMMA crown or bridge fitted on the day of surgery when the implant is stable. See same-day implants.

Materials, and what each suits

  • Zirconia

    Very strong and tooth-coloured. Our standard for single implant crowns and bridges, and for full arches in people with a strong bite. Can be monolithic (one piece) or layered with ceramic for a more lifelike front.

  • Titanium framework

    The metal backbone of a full-arch bridge, milled from titanium so it fits all the implants at once without strain.

  • PMMA

    A high-grade acrylic used for temporary teeth and for some full-arch bridges on a titanium framework. Lighter and easy to repair.

  • Ceramic fused to titanium

    Ceramic teeth layered over a titanium framework, for a natural look on a strong base.

Screwed or cemented? Wherever the implant angle allows, we screw the teeth to the implants. A screwed bridge can be removed by the dentist for cleaning or repair, and no cement is left under the gum.

How your implant teeth are made

  • 1. Record

    Scan bodies are attached to the implants and scanned with an intraoral scanner, which records each implant's exact position.

  • 2. Design

    The teeth are designed in exocad around your face, lips and bite.

  • 3. Try-in

    For a full arch, a printed try-in goes into your mouth first, so you can see the smile and we can check the bite before anything is final.

  • 4. Mill

    Zirconia is milled on the Roland mill, titanium parts on the Kelkar and Upcera mills.

  • 5. Fire and finish

    Zirconia is hardened in the sintering furnace, then stained and glazed in the ceramic furnace to match your teeth.

  • 6. Fit

    The teeth are screwed onto the implants and the bite is checked and fine-tuned.

Looking after them

Brush twice a day and clean under a bridge with a water flosser or interdental brushes. Come in every 6 months so we can check the gums, the bite and the screws. If you grind your teeth, wear a night guard.

Questions

  • What is an implant prosthesis?

    It is the part of the treatment you see and chew with: the crown, bridge or full set of teeth that is fixed onto dental implants. The implant is the titanium root in the bone, the abutment connects it, and the prosthesis is the tooth or teeth on top.

  • What is the difference between an implant crown, an implant bridge and All-on-X?

    An implant crown replaces one tooth on one implant. An implant bridge replaces several teeth on two or more implants. All-on-X replaces every tooth in a jaw with one fixed bridge on four to six implants.

  • Which material is best for teeth on implants?

    For single crowns and bridges we use zirconia, which is strong and looks natural. For a full arch, the teeth sit on a titanium framework, with zirconia or PMMA teeth, monolithic or layered zirconia, or ceramic fused to titanium. The choice depends on your bite, your budget and how the gums and lips look when you smile.

  • Is the prosthesis screwed or cemented?

    We prefer screw-retained teeth on implants wherever possible. A screwed bridge can be taken off by the dentist for cleaning or repair without damaging it, and there is no cement left under the gum.

  • Are the implant teeth made in your own lab?

    Yes. Crowns, bridges, titanium parts and full-arch teeth are designed and milled in the clinic's own lab, Titanium Arts Dental Studio, on the same floor as the clinic. The technician sees you at the try-in.

  • How much does an implant crown cost?

    A single implant with a custom abutment and a zirconia crown starts from ₹35,000, including guided surgery and an Osstem implant. All-on-X full-arch teeth start from ₹2,00,000 per jaw, including the implants and the temporary and final teeth.

  • How long do implant teeth last?

    The implant itself can last for decades with good care. The teeth on top wear like natural teeth and may need repair or replacement after 10 to 15 years. A night guard helps if you grind, and cleaning visits every 6 months keep the gums around the implants healthy.

  • Can a removable denture be fixed onto implants?

    Yes. An implant-supported denture clips onto two to four implants, so it stops moving while you eat and talk, but you can still take it out to clean it. If you want teeth that never come out, All-on-X is the fixed option.
