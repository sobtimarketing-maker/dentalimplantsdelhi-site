Immediate dental implants: can the implant go in the day the tooth comes out?

Dr Pushkar Sobti
All-on-X implantologist, Rajouri Garden, Delhi

When a tooth has to come out, most patients ask me the same thing: do I have to walk around with a gap for months? Often you don't. If the conditions are right, the implant can go in at the same visit the tooth is removed. This is called an immediate implant. In the video I explain when it works, and below are the details.

Two different things: immediate placement and immediate teeth

Immediate placement means the implant goes into the socket on the same day the tooth is taken out, instead of waiting months for the area to heal.

Immediate teeth (also called immediate loading) means a temporary tooth is fixed on the implant that same day. This is possible only when the implant is held very firmly in the bone. A patient can have the first without the second.

When an immediate implant is possible

  • The bone around the tooth is healthy and the socket walls are intact.
  • There is no active infection or pus around the root.
  • The implant can be held firmly in the bone beyond the root tip.
  • Your general health allows it, the same as for any implant. (More in who can get a dental implant.)

All of this is checked on a CBCT scan before the tooth is removed, so the decision is made in advance, not in the middle of the procedure.

When it is safer to wait

If there is an abscess, if the bone wall is badly broken, or if the implant cannot be held firmly, I let the site heal first. Sometimes the socket is grafted at the time of extraction to keep the bone from shrinking (see bone grafting for dental implants), and the implant goes in a few months later. Waiting is not a failure. It is often what makes the implant last.

Why choose an immediate implant when you can

  • One surgery instead of two, and one healing period.
  • Less bone shrinkage, because the implant goes in before the socket starts to remodel. (More in what happens to your jaw when a tooth is lost.)
  • Fewer visits and less time overall, which matters for patients travelling from outside Delhi.
  • Often no gap, when a temporary tooth can be fixed the same day.

What happens on the day

The scan and the plan are done first. On the day, under local anaesthesia, the tooth is removed gently to protect the bone around it, the implant is placed using the plan from the scan, and any gap around it is filled with graft material if needed. If the implant is firm enough, a temporary tooth is attached. Over the next few months the implant fuses with the bone, and then the final crown is made in our own lab.

Same-day teeth for the full mouth

For patients with many failing teeth, the same idea works for the whole jaw. With All-on-4 and All-on-6, the teeth are removed, implants are placed and fixed temporary teeth go on within 3 days in suitable cases. So patients usually do not have to go without teeth.

Questions patients ask

  • Can a dental implant be placed on the same day as the extraction?

    Often, yes. When the bone around the tooth is healthy, there is no active infection and the implant can be held firmly, it can go in at the same visit the tooth is removed. The CBCT scan taken before the extraction tells us whether this is possible.

  • Will I get a fixed tooth on the same day?

    Sometimes. If the implant is very stable when it is placed, a fixed temporary tooth can often be attached the same day. If it is not stable enough, we give you a temporary option that does not press on the implant and fit the fixed tooth later.

  • Is an immediate implant as strong as a regular implant?

    When the case is chosen carefully, immediate implants do well. The key is choosing the right patients: healthy bone, no active infection and good stability at placement. When those conditions are not met, waiting is the safer choice.

  • When is it better to wait after an extraction?

    When there is an active infection or abscess, when the bone wall around the tooth is badly damaged, or when the implant cannot be held firmly. Then we let the site heal first, sometimes with a graft, and place the implant later.

  • Does an immediate implant hurt?

    The extraction and implant are done in one sitting under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Many patients find it easier because there is only one surgery and one healing period instead of two.

  • How long until the final tooth is fitted?

    The implant needs a few months to fuse with the bone before the final crown or bridge is fitted. The temporary tooth, if you have one, lets you smile normally in the meantime. Your plan gives the timeline for your case.

  • Can I get a full set of teeth on the same day?

    In many full-arch cases, yes. With All-on-4 or All-on-6, failing teeth are removed, implants are placed and fixed temporary teeth go on within 3 days in suitable cases. The final teeth are made later in our own lab.
