What a single implant price should include

A tooth replaced with an implant has three parts: the implant in the bone, the abutment that connects to it, and the crown you see. Some quotes in Delhi cover only the first part, so a price that looks low can grow once the abutment and crown are added.

All my single-implant prices cover all three, plus the guided surgery used to place the implant. If you are comparing clinics, ask each one the same questions: which implant brand, whether the abutment and crown are included, whether the surgery is planned on a CBCT scan, and what the warranty is.

What changes the final cost

  • Number of implants

    A missing back tooth needs one implant. Several missing teeth can often be replaced with a bridge on two implants rather than one implant per tooth.

  • Bone grafting

    If the bone has shrunk since the tooth was lost, a graft or sinus lift may be needed first. The scan shows this before we start. Do you need a bone graft? Watch the video.

  • Implant system

    My main implant systems are Osstem CA, Hiossen NH and Nobel Active TiUltra. I will tell you which one I recommend for your case and why.

  • Crown warranty

    Crowns carry a warranty of 3 to 15 years. A higher grade of crown comes with a longer warranty.

  • Final teeth

    In full-arch cases, the material of the final bridge affects the price. Options are a titanium framework with PMMA or with zirconia, monolithic or layered zirconia, and ceramic fused to titanium.

Full mouth implants

For people missing most or all of their teeth, I usually plan All-on-4 or All-on-6: a full fixed set of teeth held by four to six implants per jaw. The back implants are often tilted into stronger bone, which lets many patients avoid a bone graft. In suitable cases you leave with fixed temporary teeth within 3 days of surgery. You can read more about how it works on the full mouth dental implants page.

Where the upper jaw has very little bone left, zygomatic or pterygoid implants can give fixed teeth without grafting. They cost more per implant but can save months of waiting. When regular implants are not possible, basal implants are another option, with fixed teeth in 72 hours.

Is an implant worth the cost?

An implant costs more at the start than a bridge or a denture. It is also the one option that doesn't rely on the neighbouring teeth and that can last decades with good care. I compared the three in more detail in implant vs bridge vs denture.
